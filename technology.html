01 / Technology — The Stack

A mesh-first sensing fabric, soldered together from first principles.

Sigilint is three layers: a DDS-based message bus over encrypted Wi-Fi mesh, a native control application, and ruggedized COTS hardware. Every layer is designed so a single operator — or a single commodity node — can be removed without the system degrading.

05 — Why this, why now
01

DDS is production-grade.

The Data Distribution Service is already trusted in naval, aerospace, and autonomous vehicle stacks. Nobody has shipped it as an opinionated, fleet-managed industrial product.

02

Commodity hardware caught up.

A $75 Raspberry Pi outperforms the industrial controllers specified in 2012 RFPs. We ruggedize the enclosure, not the silicon.

03

Sovereign tech is a mandate.

Vision 2030 and adjacent programs require locally operable, auditable stacks. Sigilint ships with a regional HQ and a trainable workforce, not a support ticket.
