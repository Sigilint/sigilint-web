01 — The Problem
Critical infrastructure is instrumented with decades-old SCADA, proprietary radios, and siloed cameras. Alerts arrive minutes late — or not at all.
01

Latency

Polling-based SCADA loops measured in seconds. Vendor radios bottleneck on a single gateway.

02

Fragility

Point-to-point topologies fail silently. A single cut link blinds entire zones of a refinery or pipeline.

03

Siloed signals

Cameras, badges, motion sensors, and drones live in separate tools. Humans are left to correlate in real time.

04

Cost of hardware

Ruggedized sensors run $3k–$20k per node. Coverage is rationed, not comprehensive.

02 — What we build

Three layers. One operational picture.

01 / MESH

Sigil Layer

DDS-based pub/sub message bus over hardened, encrypted Wi-Fi mesh. Sub-10ms delivery across zones. Self-healing. Offline-first.

02 / CONTROL

Sigil Watch

Native GUI control center. Real-time sensor and alert feed fused across cameras, badges, drones, and SCADA tags — with a single operator in the loop.

03 / HARDWARE

Sigil Node

Ruggedized COTS — Raspberry Pi, GoPro, commodity sensors, RFID readers. 10–100× cheaper than incumbent nodes. Identical to what our engineers hack on weekends.
