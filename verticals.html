03 / Verticals — Where Sigilint deploys

Hard infrastructure, hard environments.

Sigilint is opinionated: we start with verticals where latency, reliability, and environmental cost dominate the P&L — and where incumbents still sell 2012 hardware at 2032 prices. We deploy first in the Gulf and Texas.

V-001

Oil & Gas

Refineries · Pipelines · Offshore · Wells

The largest, most-instrumented — and most-fragmented — industrial vertical on earth. A single refinery runs three SCADA vendors, four camera silos, six radio brands. Operators correlate by hand.

Sigilint unifies sensing under one bus. Pipeline leak detection over distributed acoustic sensing (DAS). Refinery perimeter and badge correlation. Abandoned-well methane monitoring as a liability product for mineral lessors.

V-002

Maritime & Ports

Shipping · Marinas · Cargo · Coast

Ports are multi-tenant, multi-jurisdiction environments with partial connectivity, saltwater corrosion, and adversarial incentives. The existing stack is AIS + cameras + walkie-talkies.

Sigilint lays a resilient mesh across the quay, cranes, and vessels themselves — with cargo RFID, drone patrol, and vessel telemetry arriving in the same operator picture.

V-003

Water & Desalination

Desalination · Treatment · Distribution

Desalination is the Gulf's single most strategic infrastructure after hydrocarbons. A membrane hall going down is a national-scale incident. Yet most plants are instrumented with ~10-year-old Siemens/Schneider HMIs.

Sigilint ships a non-invasive overlay: flow, chem, and vibration telemetry plus perimeter + badging, bridged to the existing SCADA. No rip-and-replace.
